Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Nexa collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language.

1Who we are

Nexa Messenger (“Nexa”, “we”, “us”) is a messaging service for private chats, group chats, channels, stories and live voice/video rooms. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2Information we collect

We keep this deliberately small — only what the app needs to work:

  • Account details you provide: email address, display name, an optional @username and phone number, and a profile photo.
  • Content you create: messages and their attachments (photos, videos, files, voice notes), stories, group and channel posts, reactions and replies.
  • Activity signals: whether you are online, your “last seen” time, read receipts and typing indicators.
  • Device & app data: a push-notification token, your browser/platform, and the app version, used to deliver notifications and keep the app working.

We do not collect your contacts list, precise location, or the contents of your device.

3How we use it

  • Deliver, store and sync your messages, media, stories and calls across your devices.
  • Show presence, read receipts and notifications.
  • Keep the service safe — detect abuse, spam and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Provide support and fix problems.

4How your messages are stored

Chat and account data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication). Photos, videos, files and voice notes are uploaded to Cloudinary, a media hosting/CDN provider, and served from there.

One-to-one and room voice/video calls use WebRTC and are peer-to-peer — we do not record your calls. Only the small connection handshake is relayed through our servers.

5Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that run Nexa (Google Firebase, Cloudinary, our hosting and push providers), and only as needed to operate the service. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

6How long we keep it

  • Stories are deleted automatically 24 hours after posting.
  • Disappearing messages are removed after the timer you choose (24 hours, 7 days or 90 days) on both sides.
  • Other messages stay until you clear the chat or delete them.
  • When you delete your account, your profile and presence data are removed.

7Your choices and rights

  • Clear a chat or delete individual messages at any time.
  • Turn on App Lock and hide notification previews.
  • Block users you no longer want to hear from.
  • Delete your account from within the app.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to make a request.

8Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and access to chat documents is restricted to the people in that conversation by server-side rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your account credentials safe.

9Children

Nexa is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If we learn that a child has created an account, we will remove it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date above.

11Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email neuro_stack@outlook.com.
