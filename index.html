FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Nexa.

Is Nexa really free to use?
+

Yes. Creating an account and messaging are free. We're committed to keeping Nexa accessible without hidden costs or paywalls.

How secure is my data on Nexa?
+

Very secure. Nexa is built with modern authentication, encrypted data storage, and industry-standard security practices. Your conversations and personal information are protected.

Can I really use AI inside Nexa?
+

Yes. Aksify AI is built into Nexa. You can ask questions, generate content, improve your writing, translate languages, and summarize conversations without leaving the app.

Which devices and platforms are supported?
+

Nexa is available on the web (all modern browsers), with native Android support and iOS coming soon. Access it from your desktop, tablet, or phone seamlessly.

Do you have a mobile app?
+

Nexa works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on all devices. Install it from your browser in one tap for a native app-like experience with offline support and push notifications.
