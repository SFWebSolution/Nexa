❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Nexa Messenger.

Is Nexa really free to use?
+

Yes, completely! Creating an account and messaging are entirely free. We're committed to keeping Nexa accessible to everyone without hidden costs or premium paywalls.

How secure is my data on Nexa?
+

Very secure. Nexa is built with modern authentication, encrypted data storage, and industry-standard security practices. Your conversations and personal information are protected with the latest technology.

Can I really use AI inside Nexa?
+

Absolutely! Aksify AI is seamlessly integrated into Nexa. You can ask questions, generate content, improve your writing, translate languages, summarize conversations, and get intelligent assistance without leaving the app.

Which devices and platforms are supported?
+

Nexa is available on the web (all modern browsers), with native Android support and iOS coming soon. You can access Nexa from your desktop, tablet, or mobile phone seamlessly.

Do you have a mobile app?
+

Nexa works perfectly as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on all devices. You can install it from your browser with one click for a native app-like experience with offline support and push notifications.
